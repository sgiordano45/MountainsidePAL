About the program

Town basketball, played properly

Mountainside PAL Basketball fields teams from 3rd through 8th grade. Players try out in the fall, teams are selected, and the season runs through the winter with weekly practices and a league game schedule.

The Police Athletic League has served Mountainside families for decades, running youth programs that keep kids active, coached and connected to their town. Basketball sits alongside PAL's other seasonal programs, including flag football.
